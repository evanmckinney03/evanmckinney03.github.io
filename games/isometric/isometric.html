Isometric projection is a way of viewing 3D objects on a 2D screen. Objects are viewed at an angle to reveal all three coordinate axes. It is commonly used in engineering drawings and some old video games. An advantage is its simplicity and ability to show measurements, but a disadvantage is that objects do not shrink or grow in relation to the observer.

Hover over a square on the grid to highlight it. Left clicking will create a cube there. Hovering over the face of any cube will highlight it as well. Left click to place another cube there, or right click to delete the cube. Press the left or the right arrow key to rotate the underlying grid and the cubes you have placed.
